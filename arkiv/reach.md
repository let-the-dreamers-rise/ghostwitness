# Intended reach and trial status

GhostWitness is intended for recorders who may lose their phones, nearby people willing to preserve
encrypted evidence, and original owners recovering their recordings afterward.

No measured users, pilot adoption, device throughput or live cloud recovery results are claimed.
The Android debug APK builds locally; a usable integrated trial still needs configured Bee/postage,
funded Rabdi writers and real Android phones. Reward trials additionally need a deployed/funded pool
and verifier. See `../docs/online-flow.md` for reproducible setup.

After a live run, replace this section with the tested devices, exact recorder/witness/recovery steps,
available entity/reference links, observed timings and the team's maintenance plan.
